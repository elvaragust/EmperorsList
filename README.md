# EmperorsList — user manual

A free army builder and game companion for **Warhammer 40,000 (11th edition)**. It works on phones, tablets and computers, and keeps working offline.

**Open it:** https://emperorslist.ellisprelli.uk/

Used the old address (`elvaragust.github.io/EmperorsList`)? Your lists and games are still there — see **[Moving from the old address](#moving-from-the-old-address)** to bring them over in one go.

> Unofficial fan project, not affiliated with Games Workshop. Units, points and wargear come from the community **BSData** project; stratagems, enhancements, detachment rules, Core Rules and mission card text from **Wahapedia** ("Powered by Wahapedia").

---

## 1. Install it

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| iPhone / iPad | Open the link in **Safari** → Share → **Add to Home Screen** |
| Android | Open the link in **Chrome** → ⋮ menu → **Install app** |
| Windows / Mac | Open the link in **Chrome or Edge** → install icon in the address bar |

It then opens like a normal app, full screen, with its own icon. When a new version is ready the app offers **Reload** — it never reloads by itself in the middle of a game.

**First start:** all factions download in the background (a thin bar at the top shows progress). You can start using the app straight away. After that it only fetches what changed, and everything works without internet. See [Where the army data comes from](#where-the-army-data-comes-from) — the usual source is offline at the moment.

### Moving from the old address
Browsers keep each web address's data apart, so the new address starts empty. To take everything along — lists, games, collection, pins, table layouts, imported rules **and the downloaded army data**:

1. Open the **old** address (or your installed old app). Tap **Move my data** in the banner at the top (or Settings → *Moving address* → **Send my data to a new address**).
2. Tap **Send my data**. When it's sent, tap **Open the new address**, then **Bring my data here**.
3. **Installed on your home screen?** On iPhone and iPad the installed app keeps its own data, separate from Safari. Install the new address on the home screen first and open it, then Settings → *Moving address* → **Bring data from the old address** and paste the link (**Copy link** on the old address), or scan the QR code shown there.

Nothing is removed from the old address. Anything already at the new one is kept — a list or game is only replaced by a newer copy of itself, and army data only fills gaps. The parcel is encrypted on your phone; the key is only in the link, never on the server, and the parcel is deleted after 48 hours. Anyone with the link can read it, so keep it to yourself.

## 2. The five tabs

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Lists** | Your armies — as many as you like, in folders |
| **Reference** | Search every rule, unit, stratagem, enhancement and keyword; your pins |
| **Play** | The War Journal: track a game on one phone, play live across several phones (1v1, 2v2, 2v1 or three players), your results and table layouts |
| **Collection** | What you own, have built and painted |
| **Settings** | Appearance, your data, updates, backup, moving address |

## 3. Building a list

1. **Lists → New list.**
2. **Faction** — pick your army.
3. **Battle size** — Incursion (1,000), Strike Force (2,000), Onslaught (3,000) or **Custom points**.
4. **Detachments** — tap **▸** to read a detachment before choosing it; its Rules, Enhancements and Stratagems each open separately. Tick the box to choose it. The Detachment Point budget is shown at the top, and Next stays at the bottom while you scroll.
5. **Disposition** — your Force Disposition and the list's name → **Create list**.

### Your lists
- Lists are grouped in **folders**: open a list's menu on the Lists screen and type a **Folder** name. With more than a few lists, **Search lists** appears at the top.
- In a list, the **⋯** menu has **Rename**, **Battle size, detachments, disposition**, **Export text / share link / QR**, **Pinned rules and stratagems**, **Print datacards**, **Can I field this from my collection?**, **Duplicate list** and **Delete list…** (it always asks first).
- The list is split into *Characters*, *Battleline*, *Dedicated Transports*, *Other datasheets* and *Allied units* (units from another faction your army may take, e.g. Agents of the Imperium), each with its points and a **+** that opens *Add unit* filtered to that section. Under each unit's name is what it's made of (*1 Sword Brother · 6 Initiate · 4 Neophyte*).

### Adding units
- **Add unit** opens the unit list. Search by name (closest match first) or filter by role. Legends units are hidden unless you turn them on in Settings → Appearance.
- **+** adds the unit and keeps you there so you can add several; tap a unit's name to read it first (stats, models, abilities) and add it from there.
- New units start with their standard models and wargear.

### Changing a unit
Open a unit from the list. Everything is on one page, like the official app:
- **Top:** the unit's points and model count. The 📄 button jumps to the datasheet at the bottom of the page.
- **Warlord** switch (characters) and **Unit enhancements** for your chosen detachments (tap to open; pick *None* or tap the chosen one again to remove it).
- **A coloured band per kind of model** (e.g. *Sword Brother*), with + / − to change how many. Where the data lists a model once per weapon (*Initiate w/Pyreblaster*, *Initiate w/Chainsword…*), they're grouped under one *Initiate* band with the total, and each weapon variant has its own + / − underneath (+ adds a model with that loadout). A model the unit must have but is missing (e.g. the Sergeant) also gets + / − so you can add it.
- **Wargear options** under each band: what every model always carries, then each choice. With one model you tick it; with several you set **how many of those models** carry each option. + and − only change that weapon: − leaves a model without a choice (shown as *1 model has no … yet*), and + gives it to a model that has none — nothing else is swapped out behind your back.
- When a squad must all carry the same weapon, changing it on one model changes the whole unit. Options that bundle choices (e.g. *Pistol and Melee Weapon*) open their own choices underneath once picked.
- **▾ next to a weapon or enhancement** shows what it does: the weapon's profile (range, attacks, BS/WS, S, AP, D and its abilities) or the enhancement's rules text — so you can compare before you pick.
- **Weapon notes:** tap a weapon's name in the datasheet to write a note under it (e.g. *+1 attacks*). It's kept with the list and shows in games too.
- **Datasheet** at the bottom: one profile table (identical models merged), weapons, then every ability in one list — including those of a character leading the unit, tagged with its name. Who a character can join (Leader / Support) and the keywords are folded away in the last bar. The battle screen and the Reference use the same layout.
- The bar at the bottom shows the list total and whether it's valid; **Done** goes back to the list.

### Leaders
Open a Character → **Attach to a unit…** and pick from the units its Leader ability allows. The unit then shows as a **Combined** unit, with the leader's abilities listed and the ones that buff the unit marked.

### Issues
Under the points, a pill says **Valid list** or how many **problems** there are (too many points, a missing Warlord, too many copies of a unit, an option that isn't allowed…). Problems are listed right there with the reason and a button to fix each; tips that don't make the list illegal stay folded until you open them. **Start game** and **Share / export** sit just below.

**Build / Practice** at the top: Practice opens the battle screen with this list to try things out — nothing is kept, and practice games don't appear in Play. Use **Start game** for a real game.

## 4. Reading rules

- **Anything underlined is tappable** — keywords, weapon abilities, rule names and game terms inside rules text (for example *Deep Strike*, *Lethal Hits*, *surge move*). A sheet opens with the definition — including the full Core Rules.
- **Reference → search** finds everything; the coloured labels show what kind of rule each result is (Stratagem, Enhancement, Detachment rule…).
- **Browse by kind:** tap one or more of the words under the search box (*Stratagems*, *Units*, *Abilities*…) to list everything of that kind, grouped by faction. Each faction is a bar you open and close. Tap the word again, or *Clear filters*, to go back. With text in the search box they narrow the results instead.
- **Factions** are grouped under *Imperium*, *Space Marines*, *Chaos*, *Aeldari* and *Xenos* bars, so only the group you open is shown.
- **Reference → Core rules** has every Core Rules section (searchable) and the core abilities A–Z.
- **Faction pages** (Reference → a faction): *Army rules*, *Detachments* and *Datasheets*, each closed until you tap it. Inside, every detachment opens on its own (tap ▸). On datasheets, *Support* and *Leader* are listed after the unit's own abilities.

### Pins ★
Tap the **★** on a rule, stratagem, enhancement, detachment or unit to pin it. Pins appear at the top of **Reference** (and per list under ⋯ → *Pinned rules and stratagems*). During a game, pinned stratagems come first in the phase panel.

### Where the army data comes from
Units, points and wargear come from the community BSData project (`wh40k-11e`), checked for updates once a day; Settings → *Data* shows the version you have.

> **The BSData repository is currently offline.** Army data you already downloaded keeps working: it is never deleted or replaced because the source can't be reached — the app just says it's using the data saved on your device. A new browser or device can get the data by [moving it from the old address](#moving-from-the-old-address), or by entering another repository under Settings → *Advanced* → *For troubleshooting and forks* → **Source repository (for forks)**. Switching source never overwrites the data you have; switching back works offline. Keep a backup either way.

## 5. Playing a game

Play opens the **War Journal**: games in progress, your results, and the buttons below. From a list, **Start game** asks how: **On this phone**, **Host a live game** or **Join a live game**.

### On one phone
**Play → New game (this phone)** and follow the steps:
1. Your army.
2. Opponent — name, faction, and paste their list if you like.
3. Mission — your **Force Disposition** (only the ones your list's detachments allow) and your **opponent's**. Your **primary mission** then follows by itself: it's the mission on your card under their symbol. Before they reveal, the list under the pickers shows which primary each of your dispositions would give against theirs — tap one to choose it. **Pick missions by hand** lets you choose any primary (or type your own), e.g. for a twist that swaps primaries. The deployment shows a rough sketch of both zones; your saved table layouts are in the same list, and *Other* covers anything else. Then twist, **Attacker or Defender**, and who goes first. 🎲 picks at random. Each card's rules text shows under it.
4. Secondary missions — **Fixed** or **Tactical** for you and your opponent, with the rules for each. Fixed: pick 2 of the Fixed-marked cards. Tactical: your deck is shuffled and you draw in the battle.
5. Pre-battle checklist built from your list (Warlord, leaders, Deep Strike / Infiltrators / Scouts, pre-battle abilities).

### During the battle
The battle screen has six tabs — **Phase**, **Missions**, **Rules**, **Units**, **Score**, **Log** — plus a **Notes** bar you open and close (notes are kept for the whole game).

- **Next** (bottom bar) moves through the phases and turns. **CP is not added automatically:** add your Command phase CP with the CP buttons. If someone forgot to press Next, tap the turn banner to hand the turn over (it asks first). **R1–R5** at the top jump to the start of a round.
- **Phase:** your army's stratagems that can be used in this phase and turn (pinned ★ ones first — tap the CP button to spend), and your abilities that mention this phase.
- **Missions:** every card shows its rules (flavour text left out). The parts that score this battle round are marked *This round*, the others dimmed. Tick each scoring line (or count it with − / + for "for each…" lines; caps are applied; ticking an *or* line clears its alternative), then **Add … VP** puts it in that round's score. A scored primary part is locked for the round — **Enable again** to score it once more. Secondaries score as you tick them: the VP is added at once and taken off again if you untick.
  - **Tactical:** at the start of your Command phase open **Secondaries** and tap **Draw secondary** (one card per tap). Tap a card in your hand to put it back in the deck, or a card in the deck to take it into your hand. Scored (✓) and discarded (struck through) cards stay listed in their round.
  - Your opponent's primary and secondaries are shown underneath, read only.
- **Rules:** your army rules (e.g. *Oath of Moment*) and each of your detachments' rules, each in a bar you open. Where Wahapedia has the same rule its (usually newer) text is shown.
- **Units:** each unit with its models left and its points. Tap a unit for its **shots grid** — one column per weapon with the total shots/attacks in the header, one row per kind of model ("3×"). Tap a row to see each model: **− / +** tracks its wounds and losing the last one removes it; **+** also goes past full health (5/4) when a rule gives extra wounds. A weapon that only removed models carried is greyed out and comes back with the model. **Opponent's units:** paste their list at setup (in a live game it's shared automatically) and tap any of their units for a read-only datasheet and shots grid.
- **Score:** primary and secondary VP per round.
- **Log:** everything that happened.
- **Your list in a game:** a game keeps its own copy of your army from when it started. Edit the list mid-game and the game asks whether to **use the changed list** (in a live game the others see it too) or **keep the game's army**. Finished games always keep the army they were played with.
- Opening a unit and coming back keeps your place, and dragging down at the top of the screen doesn't reload the app.

**End of the game:** a big *Victory / Defeat / Draw*, both scores with the winner marked, by how much, and the score round by round. **Correct the score** fixes numbers — both sides' — but only on your phone (in a live game each player can correct their own view). **Back to the game's score** undoes it, and reopening the game clears it. **Round by round replay** plays the battle back over the five rounds: switch between **Victory points** (the running total) and **Battle wounds** (how much of each army's starting wounds is still on the table, so any two armies compare fairly). Pause, replay, or tap a round to stop there. In a game on one phone only your own losses are known. Finished games are listed with a coloured Win / Loss / Draw badge, the score and who won.

**Games that go quiet:** a game with no changes for **2 hours** (from anyone, in a live game) is ended automatically. Nothing is lost: it stays under *In progress* marked *Ended after 2 hours*. Open it and choose **Resume game** to carry on exactly where you were (a live game reconnects; the other phones resume too when they open it), or **Keep it finished** to file it with your results.

### Live across phones (1v1, 2v2, 2v1 or three players)
- **Host:** Play → **Host live game** → your name and army. A room code and QR code appear.
- **Others:** scan the QR with the phone camera, or Play → **Join** and type the code, then their name and army.
- The host chooses **1v1**, **2v2**, **2v1** (the pair can be either team) or **3 players** (everyone for themselves, taking turns in order), sorts players into teams, sets deployment and twist, and starts. Each team picks one **Force Disposition** — the choices are what either teammate's list allows — and its primary follows from the other side's disposition (or pick it by hand). Each team chooses Fixed or Tactical secondaries together, and either side can record the roll-off as *We attack* / *We defend*.
- **Teammates share CP:** one pool per team that either teammate spends from.

What's shared: whose turn it is and the current phase, everyone's CP, every team's score, each team's primary and secondary cards (your teammate manages them with you; opponents can read them), the log, and how many models (and points) each player's units have left.

- **Look around freely** — the phase words under the header only change *your* screen (the current phase has a •). Only the team whose turn it is (or the host) moves the game on.
- **You can only change your own (or your team's) CP, score and missions.** You see theirs update as they change them.
- **Connection:** phones talk through the app's server, which only passes messages on and stores nothing. Everyone needs internet during the game, and the host's game must stay open. If a phone's connection silently dies (screen locked, Wi-Fi to mobile data), both sides notice within seconds and reconnect by themselves.
- **Lost the host?** Keep playing: everything works on your phone (phases, turn, CP, score, missions). When the host is back, your phone sends what you changed: your team's CP, score, missions and disposition, and the turn if you got further. The other team's numbers always come from the host, so nothing of theirs is overwritten.
- **The host's phone is gone for good** (battery, broken)? Game menu → **Carry on as host**: your phone becomes the host under a new room code, with everything it had. The others open their Game menu → **Follow the game to a new room** and type the code; what they changed meanwhile comes along.
- **End the game:** the host ends it for everyone (Game menu → **End the game for everyone**); others can **End the game on my phone** to save the result and stop following. A phone that was off at the end gets the result the next time both apps are open.
- **Turn off live changes** (Game menu): unlinks your phone. You keep everyone's army and score as they were and can change anything yourself. **Reconnect to the live game** joins again.

### Table layouts
**Play → Table layouts** stores photos or screenshots of terrain layouts. They appear in the deployment list when you set up a game.

## 6. Sharing and importing lists

From a list's **⋯** menu:
- **Export text / share link / QR** — text in the same layout as the official Warhammer app (copy or download), and a share link and QR code that open the list in EmperorsList on another device.
- **Print datacards** — one card per unit; print or save as PDF.

**Import:** Lists → **Import** → paste a list exported from the official app. The faction, detachments and Force Disposition are found wherever they are in the header (if there's no disposition line, the detachment's one is used). Units, models, wargear, Warlord and enhancements are matched by name. Models the datasheet requires but the text left out (e.g. a Sergeant) are added, and everything that couldn't be matched or was added is listed so you can check it.

## 7. Collection

Pick a faction and enter how many models you own, have built and have painted. From a list's ⋯ menu, **Can I field this from my collection?** compares the list with your collection.

## 8. Settings

- **Appearance** — colour theme (*Follow my army* or *Always one theme*); text size (Normal / Large / Extra large); colours (Dark / Light / Match phone); title font (Gothic / Engraved / Plain); Legends units in Add unit (Hidden / Shown); layout on laptops and tablets (*Fit the screen* / *Phone column*).
- **Your data on this device** — how many lists, games and pins are stored, how much space is used, and whether the browser has agreed to keep it.
- **Data** — check for updates (also happens daily by itself; only files that changed are downloaded).
- **Backup** — **Download backup** saves all lists, games, collection, pins and layouts to a file; **Restore from file** loads it on any device (nothing already there is deleted).
- **Moving address** — send everything to a new address, or bring it in from the old one ([how](#moving-from-the-old-address)).
- **Advanced** → *For troubleshooting and forks* — rule label style; **Source repository (for forks)**; *Extra rules (Wahapedia)*: **Update from Wahapedia**, **Import CSV files** or **Clear imported**; *Live games*: an optional own PeerJS server, only if you'd rather phones talk to each other directly.

**Where your data is kept.** Lists, games, pins, collection, layouts and the downloaded game data are stored in the browser's own database (IndexedDB) for this web address, on this device only — there are no accounts. That means:
- Each browser and each web address has its own copy: Chrome and Samsung Internet on the same phone don't share lists, and neither do the old and new addresses (see [Moving](#moving-from-the-old-address)). On Android the installed app shares Chrome's data; on iPhone and iPad the installed app has its own, separate from Safari.
- Clearing the browser's site data, or uninstalling it, deletes everything. A browser can also clear it by itself when the device is low on space — the app asks the browser to keep it (Settings → *Your data on this device* shows whether it agreed). **Safari on iPhone/iPad deletes a site's data after 7 days without a visit** unless it's installed on the home screen — install it.
- It is **not** the browser cache: clearing *cached images and files* doesn't touch it; clearing *site data* / *cookies and site data* for the site does.
- **Keep a backup** now and then: Lists reminds you every two weeks (every five days in Safari on iPhone/iPad). **Save a backup** opens the share sheet so the file can go straight to Google Drive, iCloud Files or email (elsewhere it downloads).

## 9. Questions

**Is it free?** Yes, no accounts and no ads.

**Does it work offline?** Yes, after the first start. Live games need internet.

**A live game said "connected" but changes didn't arrive?** That was a bug in older versions: a phone whose connection died silently kept showing as connected. Tap **Reload** when the app offers the new version; connections are now checked every few seconds and reconnect by themselves.

**I cleared my browser and the army data won't download.** The BSData source is offline (see [Where the army data comes from](#where-the-army-data-comes-from)). Restore your backup file for your lists; the army data can come from another address with *Moving address*, or from another source repository.

**Why is a unit or rule missing or wrong?** The data comes from the community and may not be updated yet — Settings → *Data* → check for updates.

**Where do the Core Rules, stratagems and mission cards come from?** Wahapedia's pages and exports, fetched when the app is built and refreshed daily.

**Can I use it on my PC?** Yes — open the link in a browser, or install it from Chrome/Edge. Settings → Appearance → *Layout on laptops and tablets* → **Fit the screen** uses the whole window.
